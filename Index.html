I Wanna Be Yours

To my whole world,

Seeing the birthday surprise you built for me left me speechless. Every detail, every word, and all the love you poured into it made me feel like the luckiest person alive.

Just like the song says—in every quiet moment and every loud laugh, I just wanna be yours. Thank you for making my birthday and my life so beautiful.

— Forever yours, Sonu 💗
